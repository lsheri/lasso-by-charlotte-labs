# Brand

## Lasso, the default skin

- The existing lockup stays; only its colour moved to acid (14 Oct 2026).
- The "you are here" cue is a sunk fill with weight 600. The settings rail and Ask tabs keep the 2.5px acid rule (`--here-rule`, `--here-weight`).
- Ask's own green is the landing lime (`--ask-live`, #04f85b), and acid stays the highlight everywhere else.

## Never rename

`--lasso-*` tokens, the `lasso_*` MCP tools, PostHog events, the repo, Supabase and the MCP URL keep their names whatever the skin.

## Voice

Lasso describes work, never workers. No scores about people, and no audit or surveillance words in copy. No em dashes.

## Charlotte (second skin, Storybook only)

Exhibit direction (12 Nov 2026): white paper, navy ink, lime only when something is live. Values live in `src/design/skins/charlotte.css`; switch the Skin menu to Charlotte, or open **Foundations / Skins** to see both side by side with contrast.

| Job | Charlotte | Contrast on white |
|---|---|---|
| Text, focus, state, highlight, the "on the record" mark | navy `#0B2A4A` | 14.5:1 |
| Secondary text | `#55697A` | 5.7:1 |
| Muted text | `#73859A` | 3.8:1 |
| Selection | navy at 10% on white | text stays 12:1 |
| Live (Ask, live dots, link underlines) | lime `#04F85B` | 1.44:1, so fill and decoration only |

Lime never carries focus, text or state. A link is navy text with a lime underline, never lime alone. A test fails if a token edit breaks either rule.

### The mark: Monoline C, Thin

See **Brand / Charlotte mark**. Storybook only until PR6 gives LassoThinkingMark a Charlotte branch.

- **Idle** is the still C: an open arc (gap from 315 to 45 degrees) with three spokes and an ink hub.
- **Reading** grows the web on a 6.4s cycle: 4.4s of growth, a hold, then a fade from 6.0s to 6.4s. The gap closes between 0.3 and 0.7 of the growth, two rings draw in, and the hub pulses.
- **Live** is the C with the lime hub.
- At 24px and below the strokes get heavier, and the web drops its nodes and outer ring.
- One animation loop writes SVG attributes directly, so React does not re-render each frame. It runs only while reading and on screen. Under reduced motion the mark shows the finished web, still.
- Colours follow the skin: ink is `--text-primary`, the hub is `--live`.
- The lapel tile (future app icon and avatar) uses fixed brand colours. The suggest-workstreams version (open C, three dots) waits on G6.
- Source: `src/design/brand/`.

## A second skin

A skin is a parameter: `data-skin` on the page, overriding the job-named tokens in `tokens.css`. Its glyphs, mark and motion are built in Storybook first, and nothing in the app imports them until a reviewed pull request does.
