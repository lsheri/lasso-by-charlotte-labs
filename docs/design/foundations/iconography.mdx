# Iconography

Every glyph is in **Foundations / Icons**, read from `ICON_NAMES` so a new glyph appears there without a story change.

## GraphiteIcon

- 42 glyphs on one 20px grid (`viewBox 0 0 20 20`), stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, `currentColor`.
- Source: `src/components/notebook/icons.tsx`. The glyph count is pinned by the pass84 test, and two glyph shapes by icon1.
- Each glyph has a signature: one stroke that draws when it plays. `animate` turns it on and `playOnMount` plays it once on mount. Reduced motion stills it.
- A glyph with a `title` is an image with that label. Without one it is hidden from assistive technology, and its control carries the label.

## Two languages today

`lucide-react` is imported in 93 files and GraphiteIcon is used in 33. Lucide sits on a 24 grid, so a 1.5 stroke at 12px renders under 1px. Small Lucide icons keep stroke 2. One glyph map per skin is the plan; see `docs/design/debt.md`.

## Charlotte (Exhibit) glyphs

See **Foundations / Charlotte glyphs**. Since PR4, GraphiteIcon draws this set in the app when the skin is Charlotte. Lasso renders a byte-identical SVG for all 42 glyphs (tested).

- Grammar: 20 grid with a 15px live area, stroke 1.5, 2px corners, one idea per glyph, ink only, no motion in chrome, sized 12, 16, 18 or 20.
- The same 42 names, keyed by `GraphiteIconName`, on the same 20px grid with stroke 1.5. Source: `src/design/icons/charlotte.ts`.
- 10 Charlotte-only names (sign-out, pin, lock, trash, edit, link, download, info, bell, submit) in `charlotte-extra.ts`. They stay out of `ICON_NAMES` until PR4b gives each a Lasso path too.
- 12 work-kind glyphs in 6 families in `charlotte-kinds.ts`. Family colours wait for the `--t-*` tokens.
- The section outline is dashed (2.1 on, 1.9 off). It is marked as data, and drawn without `pathLength`, because `pathLength` rescales the dash and would draw it solid.
- A test checks every precomputed rounded rectangle and circle against the spec's formulas, and fences these files out of the app.

## Rules

- Real vendor marks wherever a surface represents that vendor, never a hue dot standing in for one.
- Board hue tints the board glyph only.
- Icon-only controls always carry an aria-label and a tooltip.
